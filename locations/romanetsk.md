---
title: Romanetsk
---
Romanetsk is a bustling metropolis where money comes first and life is cheap. Cosmopolitan and crowded, the city draws innumerable visitors from across the region.

Romanetsk was founded so long ago that nobody can remember who did it – but everyone knows why. The city has an accessible harbor, frequented by merchants as often as it is by pirates. Longshoremen unload barrels of cursed cod, moon carp, ember snappers, and crimson tuna onto the docks every day. Goods leave the city via a variety of trade routes than fan out into the vast steppes and mountain ranges beyond, and the deep Karsk river carries flat-bottomed riverboats loaded high with grains and mineral resources into the city.

The city is deeply stratified, both physically and socially. Wealthy aristocrats live in tall, ivy-covered buildings on the river’s edge with flower-covered balconies looking down at the busy docks below and at the endless sea beyond. An average person might find work in the many trading industries in the city, or in the inns and taverns serving hungry laborers and sailors at the water’s edge. The city’s poor live happy but cramped lives at or just below ground level, the poorest of them all living in basement rooms that occasionally flood during major storms.
