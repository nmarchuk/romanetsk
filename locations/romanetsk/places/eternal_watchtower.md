---
title: The Eternal Watchtower
---
After a horrible invasion nearly wiped out the city three hundred years ago, a strange and powerful warlock known as the **Maker** made a pact with a dark god to protect the city from invaders. He used this power to construct a terrible tower, the **Eternal Watchtower**, in the city center, and since then the city has been safe from invasion. In fact, this has been so successful that other cities have constructed their own Eternal Watchtowers.

The tower, made of black shining bricks and covered in thick vines, is so tall that it extends past the clouds. When the warlock died of old age, he left instructions for the keeping of the watchtower: The city shall choose three souls to work in service of the tower: the **Watcher**, the **Keeper**, and the **Follower**.

What their function is a highly-guarded secret, but this much is known: the Watcher remains in the tower at all times. The Keeper stands guard over the Watcher and of the tower itself – they prevent anyone from entering and leaving. The Follower remains near the tower and trains to become the next Watcher, ready at a moment’s notice to become the next Watcher should the current one die or leave. The Watcher, Keeper, and Follower are paid handsomely for their service and for their secrecy.
