# Letter sent to Romanetsk’s notarization office, sent at expedited speed:

To Whom it May Concern,

I hope this message finds you well. 

I write with the intention to correct an egregious oversight I have made, should that at all be possible. Due to my employment as a Level 4 tax collector, familial matters of forms and legal procedure are often placed under my care. Such was the case involving endangered species permits I was presumed to have submitted on behalf of my brother, Ilya Ivashin. However, since I have been away on official business relating to pressing occupational duties, I did not see this request from him, and consequently did not file the proper forms in time. Ilya, having no knowledge that I had been called away from Romantesk, and thus no reason to think that the proper forms had not been filed as they have always been for prior trips of this sort, proceeded forward in error.

I sincerely apologize for the confusion, and bear any responsibility for the consequences that have befallen my brother due to my incompetence. If there is any chance these forms can be backdated, I would be incredibly appreciative. Any associated fees can be collected from the Ivashin estate. I am currently in the process of making my swift return back to Romanetsk, but if any questions arise in the meantime, please contact Evrart Guzman. Having dedicated myself to working under him with the utmost fervor, I trust that he will serve as a guarantor of my usual proper conduct and professionalism.

Sincerely,
Zhenya Ivashin, ID [ ]

# Letter Received

Zhenya,

We are pleased to here of the incoming tax exemption for the family. Certainly this will be of use to us. We are sure that, since you decided to write to us about this, it will be forthcoming before the next festival, in a few weeks.

Please immediately send 300 GP to the address of 10 Bookend St, Romanetsk. While we were in the process of sorting out the family finances, your brother was caught in a similar misunderstanding related to nonpayment of taxes for killing endangered creatures. This is, of course, nonsense. His hunting trip with Uncle Zistend was perfectly legal and necessary for your brother's mental health during these trying times.

I am sure that your salary will cover the sum. If, for some reason, you've spent frivilously on your accomodations during this excursion of yours, you'll remember that there's a little village along the Riksgransen River near the foot of the Gorn Range that our family has an interest in. The owner of the logging company, Paco, long ago sold us a 50% stake in his business. However, he has claimed for several years that the business makes no profit, and thus he has no money to send us. Perhaps you'll show him that knife of yours.

As for news from the city? Well, we can catch up over dinner when you get back. We had to give the servants a temporary vacation. Your friend Tanka Jahari has graciously offered to help feed the family cats.

Father and Mother
