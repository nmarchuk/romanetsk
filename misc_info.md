# Special Abilities

While in the Hall of Ledgers or other tax-collecting offices, sworn Tax Collectors such as yourself and others have access to the following abilities:
- ***Legal Recall***. A creature within 30 feet of you that you can see must make a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw or be forced to both remember and understand a single element of the tax code of your choosing.
- ***Freeze Assets***. Choose a creature within 30 feet of you. All the money they are carrying becomes temporarily very heavy. The creature has a movement speed of 0 until they drop the money.
- ***Bureaucracy***. Make a DC 15 Intimidation check on a creature within 30 feet of you that you can see. On a success, the creature is mechanically bored by your knowledge of the code. They lose Concentration and have a -2 penalty to all Dexterity checks until they leave your sight or the tax collecting office.
